Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you commit.

Still have questions? Ask us about your account.

Is the Google Ads budget included in your fee?

No. Google advertising spend is separate from Adnilo's management fee and is paid to Google.

Can you work with an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We can review an existing account, identify tracking and campaign issues, and recommend whether to optimise or rebuild parts of it.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

No reputable agency can guarantee a fixed number of qualified enquiries. Results depend on competition, budget, location, demand and your website.

How will I know what is working?

We focus on measurable actions such as calls and forms, alongside spend and campaign performance. Tracking limitations are explained rather than hidden.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. We review your current pages first and recommend landing-page changes only where they are useful.
