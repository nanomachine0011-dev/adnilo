Lead system

FAQ

Questions about Local Lead Generation.

Which industries can Adnilo help?

Adnilo is built for local service businesses such as cleaning companies, dentists, trades, clinics, gyms and similar local service brands.

Is local lead generation only Google Ads?

No. Google Ads is often central, but landing pages, tracking and follow-up matter too.

Do you guarantee booked jobs?

No. We do not make fake guarantees. We build and improve the system that helps create more useful calls and enquiries.

Can you start with an audit first?

Yes. An audit is often the safest first step when campaigns or tracking already exist.
